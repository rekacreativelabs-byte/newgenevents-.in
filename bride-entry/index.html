Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from brides planning their wedding entry choreography.

I am not a dancer. Can I walk gracefully without feeling awkward?

Yes, absolutely! Over 80% of our brides are complete non-dancers. Prachi Rana designs natural, poised movement sequences focusing on walking cadence, bridal lehenga weight management, posture, and eye contact rather than complicated dance steps.

What are the most popular bridal entry concepts for Indian weddings?

The top concepts include the Floral Phoolon Ki Chadar Walk escorted by brothers, the Royal Heritage Palki Procession, the Sacred Ganga Aarti entry with traditional lamps and flute score, and the Majestic Entry with synchronized cold pyrotechnics.

How much does bridal entry choreography and production cost?

Concepts start from ₹10,000+ for essential couple and solo bridal choreography and custom audio medley editing. Full-scale productions with professional dance troupes, props, and on-site stage direction are quoted transparently based on destination requirements.

How far in advance should we book our bridal entry?

For prime wedding dates (October through February) across Delhi NCR, Jaipur, Udaipur, and Goa, we recommend reserving your date 4 to 8 weeks in advance to ensure personalized music production and rehearsal scheduling.
