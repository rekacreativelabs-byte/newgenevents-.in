Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about planning your grand couple entry.

What is the difference between a romantic couple entry and an enthusiastic couple act?

A romantic couple entry focuses on intimate, slow-paced elegance with floral arches, graceful spotlights, emotional voiceovers, and gentle slow-dance choreography. An enthusiastic couple act features the bride and groom dancing at the center surrounded by energetic dancers in coordinated red and gold costumes, dazzling stage lights, and high-energy music drops.

Can couples with zero dancing experience perform a couple entry act?

Yes! Over 80% of our couples are complete beginners. Prachi Rana designs natural, comfortable movements that look stylish, regal, and effortless without requiring difficult acrobatics or stressful rehearsal routines.

Do you edit our couple entrance music and story voiceovers?

Yes, every concept includes custom studio sound engineering with smooth beat transitions, personalized couple voice recordings, and timed drops for cold pyrotechnics and stage spotlights.

What are the costs for couple entry choreography?

Essential couple entry choreography and custom music editing start from ₹10,000+. Elaborate stage acts with professional dancer troupes, cold pyrotechnics, and on-site stage direction are quoted transparently based on your event scale.
