Frequently Asked Questions

Essential guidance on organizing your groom entry and baraat transition.

What are the most popular groom entry ideas for destination weddings?

Top concepts include vintage open-top convertible arrivals, Royal Rajputana sword & dhol nagada processions, quad bike / ATV grand arrivals, and coordinated groom squad flashmob routines where the groom dances with his closest friends.

I am not comfortable dancing on stage. Can I still have an impactful groom entry?

Absolutely! Nick (Mr. Lock) and Prachi Rana design swagger-based entry walks, grand car positioning, and troupe synchronization that create jaw-dropping royal impact without requiring complex dance choreography from the groom.

How are sound cues and baraat transitions coordinated?

We produce custom high-energy audio medleys that transition seamlessly from the live street baraat dhol into the stage venue speakers with timed bass drops and pyrotechnic spotlights.

What is the cost of groom entry choreography and troupe direction?
