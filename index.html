Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Clear answers about booking dates, rehearsal formats, destination travel, and our visual catalogue concepts.

What are the starting prices for wedding entry concepts?

Our official Visual Wedding Entry Catalogue concepts start at ₹10,000+ (such as Sacred Aarti Entry, Royal Welcome, Ganga-Inspired Moment, and Bridal Processions). Complete sangeet packages and multi-troupe productions are tailored based on city, troupe size, and rehearsals.

Neither of us is a trained dancer. Can we still perform gracefully?

Over 80% of our couples are complete beginners! Prachi Rana’s signature method specializes in choreographing natural, comfortable movements that look regal, stylish, and effortless on stage without any rehearsal anxiety.

Who directs destination weddings in Udaipur, Jaipur & Goa?

Our core leadership team—including founder Prachi Rana, Sam (Mr. House) who manages destination weddings with complete planning-to-execution ownership, and Nick (Mr. Lock) with 11 years of professional stage experience—personally travels to coordinate on-site rehearsals and live stage execution.

Do you create custom audio tracks and voiceovers?

Yes. Every concept includes custom sound engineering with smooth beat transitions, personalized couple voiceovers, and timed climax drops tailored to your stage spotlights and pyrotechnics.
