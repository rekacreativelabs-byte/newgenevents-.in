From Closing Deals To Creating Memories

I’m a sales and business development professional turned entrepreneur, with rich experience across FinTech, EdTech, and professional learning. Over the years, I’ve worked with leading organizations including 1West, Testbook, and BYJU’S—handling complete sales cycles, qualifying high-value opportunities, negotiating terms, and driving deal closures.

At 1West, I worked as a Funding/Lending Specialist, negotiating terms and closing MCA funding deals. At Testbook, I drove revenue across high-ticket technical learning products (Full Stack Development, Data Science, and Digital Marketing). During my time at BYJU’S Exam Prep, I contributed to ₹15 Lakh+ in revenue across dynamic customer interactions.

Beyond sales numbers, these experiences taught me something fundamental: I became genuinely curious about how businesses work. How do they acquire customers? What makes people trust a brand? And most importantly—how do you turn a transaction into an experience people remember for a lifetime?

Today, as CEO and Founder of NewGen Events, I bring that curiosity and strategic rigour into entrepreneurship. Our vision is to celebrate Indian culture, preserve sacred traditions, and create emotionally meaningful experiences for couples and families. Because a wedding is not just an event—it is a collection of emotions, traditions, relationships, stories, and memories that people carry for a lifetime.

With a leadership team including Sam (Mr. House) who has directed destination weddings from Udaipur to Jaipur, and Nick (Mr. Lock) with 11 years as a professional dancer performing at celebrity celebrations like Jennifer Winget's wedding, Nupur Sanon, and Mira Rajput, we guarantee an unforgettable performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Clear answers about booking dates, rehearsal formats, destination travel, and our visual catalogue concepts.

How is pricing tailored for wedding entry concepts?

Every NewGen Events experience is custom-tailored to your unique vision. Pricing is quoted privately on a 1-on-1 basis based on your destination city, troupe size, rehearsal format, and production requirements.

Neither of us is a trained dancer. Can we still perform gracefully?

Over 80% of our couples are complete beginners! Prachi Rana’s signature method specializes in choreographing natural, comfortable movements that look regal, stylish, and effortless on stage without any rehearsal anxiety.

Who directs destination weddings in Udaipur, Jaipur & Goa?

Our core leadership team—including founder Prachi Rana, Sam (Mr. House) who manages destination weddings with complete planning-to-execution ownership, and Nick (Mr. Lock) with 11 years of professional stage experience—personally travels to coordinate on-site rehearsals and live stage execution.

Do you create custom audio tracks and voiceovers?

Yes. Every concept includes custom sound engineering with smooth beat transitions, personalized couple voiceovers, and timed climax drops tailored to your stage spotlights and pyrotechnics.
