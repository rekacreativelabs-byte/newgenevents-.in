Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding wedding sangeet rehearsals and audio mixing.

How do you teach family members who live in different cities or countries?

We provide password-protected, high-definition step-by-step video tutorials with front-and-back views and counts. We also conduct live Zoom rehearsal sessions followed by intensive 1-2 day on-site bootcamps at your destination before the sangeet night.

Can elders and parents perform comfortably without tiring steps?

Yes! Prachi Rana specializes in graceful, nostalgic choreography for parents and grandparents featuring seated sequences, slow traditional hand mudras, and heartwarming classic Bollywood melodies that look elegant and feel completely effortless.

Do you edit custom audio tracks and sound transitions?

Every package includes custom studio sound engineering with seamless song-to-song transitions, family voice greetings, and balanced audio leveling for live stage acoustics.

What is the typical pricing for wedding sangeet choreography?

Essential choreography concepts start from ₹10,000+. Complete multi-performance packages covering couple, parents, friends, and finale medleys are quoted transparently based on total routines and rehearsal format.
