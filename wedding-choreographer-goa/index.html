Frequently Asked Questions • Goa

Answers to questions about beach wedding entries, sand footwear, and destination rehearsals.

What type of entries work best on Goa beach lawns and sand?

For Goa weddings, we specialize in barefoot or wedge-friendly sunset walks with tropical floral arches, cold sparkular walkways, ATV quad bike arrivals, and energetic LED percussionist troupe accompaniment that seamlessly rolls into the afterparty.

Which beachside resort venues do you serve in Goa?

We direct wedding performances and entries across South and North Goa: W Goa (Vagator), Taj Exotica (Benaulim), ITC Grand Goa (Arossim), Alila Diwa (Majorda), Caravela Beach Resort, and Grand Hyatt Goa.

How do you coordinate rehearsals for guests flying into Goa from different cities?

We distribute step-by-step tutorial videos beforehand, followed by an energetic 1-day pool party or pre-sangeet rehearsal bootcamp on-site in Goa, ensuring everyone is synchronized and excited.

What are the costs for Goa destination wedding choreography?

Core choreography concepts start from ₹10,000+. Complete multi-event destination packages covering beach entry, sangeet, and sundowner afterparty choreography are quoted transparently based on your event scale.
