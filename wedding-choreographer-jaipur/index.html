Frequently Asked Questions • Jaipur

Details about destination wedding choreography and on-site rehearsals in Jaipur.

Who travels to direct destination weddings in Jaipur?

Our leadership team personally travels to Jaipur: founder Prachi Rana, Sam (Mr. House) who manages destination weddings with complete planning-to-execution ownership, and Nick (Mr. Lock) who coordinates on-site rehearsals and live stage execution.

Which luxury palace venues do you serve in Jaipur?

We direct wedding entries and sangeet productions at Fairmont Jaipur, Rambagh Palace, Jai Mahal Palace, Samode Palace, The Leela Palace Jaipur, and Shiv Vilas.

How are rehearsals handled for destination weddings in Jaipur?

We begin with customized video tutorials for couples and families beforehand, followed by an intensive 1 to 2-day on-site choreography bootcamp and stage spacing rehearsal right at your Jaipur palace resort.

What are popular entry concepts for palace weddings in Jaipur?

Popular palace concepts include the Royal Welcome with Rajasthani folk dhol and lancers, Sacred Ganga Aarti devotional moments, and high-energy customized couple acts with dancers in royal red and gold costumes.
