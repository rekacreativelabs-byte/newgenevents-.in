How are lakeside wedding entries coordinated in Udaipur?

At waterfront venues like Jagmandir and Lake Pichola resorts, we coordinate bridal and couple boat arrivals with synchronized jetty spotlights, live flute & sitar audio cues, and royal flower showers as the couple steps onto the marble promenade.
