Price Drivers

5 Key Factors That Determine Wedding Entry Cost

Understanding where your investment goes ensures you get maximum value and visual impact on stage.

Troupe Size & Costuming

More dancers require wardrobe customization, synchronized rehearsals, and choreography spacing for your venue's stage width.

Studio Audio Engineering

Custom beat-matching, voiceover mastering, and cinematic transition drops require dedicated studio production time.

Stage Pyrotechnics & Props

Indoor-safe cold sparkulars, heavy dry-ice low fog machines, brass aarti lamps, or royal palki setups affect production costs.

Destination Travel & Lodging

Destination celebrations in Jaipur, Udaipur, or Goa require team transit, local rehearsal slots, and on-site lodging coordination.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about wedding choreography budgets and booking terms.

What is the starting price for wedding entry choreography in India?

At NewGen Events, essential couple or solo entry choreography concepts start from ₹10,000+. This includes tailored choreography step design for beginners and custom studio sound editing.

Are there any hidden fees or travel surcharges?

No. We believe in 100% price transparency. Travel and accommodation for destination weddings (such as Jaipur, Udaipur, or Goa) are itemized upfront in your written proposal with no unexpected last-minute additions.

How much does a full troupe production entry cost?

Full-scale productions with 6 to 16 professional dancers in coordinated red and gold costumes, live stage management, and props are quoted on a customized 1-on-1 basis depending on troupe size, location, and event scope.

How does NewGen Events customize packages for non-dancers?

Over 80% of our clients are non-dancers. Every package is customized around your natural comfort, posture, and wedding attire, so you don't waste budget on complex technical routines you won't enjoy.
